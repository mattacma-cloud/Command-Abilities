# Command-Abilities
Mod for HBS BattleTech / BEXT providing additional Commander only battlefield abilities.

Command Abilities gives your player commander three combat orders. You can tell NPC allied lances to concentrate fire on one enemy, send them sprinting to a point on the map, or plant and try to break a rattled enemy MechWarrior into ejecting. Allied lances may refuse an order and your own lance is not affected by the abilities. Using any of the three abilities puts all of them on cooldown for 4 rounds.

This mod was designed to support players undertaking the Lore Pack missions, which often have large, supportign aliied forces.

**Overview**

This mod is for the player commander only; It is not a skill-tree pick and is never granted to the AI. In combat, three extra buttons sit on the MechWarrior tray and using any of them spends your firing action for that activation. Using any one prevents all three from activating until the 4 round cooldown has completed.

Allied NPC lances hear both Priority Target and Priority Move. Each lance answers through its lance leader: and the ability is tested via their Tactics against your Commander's. A successful roll means that whole lance carries out the order. A failure means they ignore you and fight their own fight. Named pilots in the lance, when the game knows them, are the ones who answer, keeping with existing mission hierarchy. Radio barks play in the centre conversation frame, so you can see who agreed and who thinks your handle on tactical manoeuvre leaves something to be desired.

**Priority Target** marks one enemy in sensor range. The mark lasts for the rest of this round and all of the next. It does not strip evasion and does not make the shot easier. Lances that obey must attack that target if they can engage it; they will not dump leftover fire onto someone else. Lances that refuse the order will keep their own targets. 

**Priority Move** is a command ping: a point in visual range and line of sight of the Commander's 'Mech. Obeying lances sprint toward it for the rest of this round and all of the next, not firing while they run. The same Tactics test decides who moves and who continues to operate independently.

**Intimidation** is a finishing shout, not a general-purpose debuff. You must remain stationary, spend Resolve, and pick an enemy 'Mech within close range and line of sight whose pilot is already rattled (Unsettled, Stressed, or Panicked). A Confident ace cannot be selected, even if the machine is wrecked or on the ground. A successful roll against your target forces an ejection. Failure feeds Resolve to the enemy team, as they refuse your intimidation an rally against you. Attempting against named heroes who would never break still costs the attempt and will still steel the enemy’s nerve. This ability can only be used once per combat. 

The three orders share a cooldown period, so you choose: massed fire, a reposition, or a shout. You cannot chain them.

**Depends On**
Requires ModTek and Abilifier. Intimidation also requires PanicSystem. Best with Mission Control allied lances and Better AI.
